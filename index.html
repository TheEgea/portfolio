About me

Final-year Computer Engineering student at Tecnocampus (UPF), wrapping up my degree with a thesis on ethical pentesting and offensive/defensive cybersecurity labs.

I'm passionate about security from every angle — networks, systems, web apps and digital forensics. Outside of university, I run my own HomeLab on Proxmox where I experiment with virtualisation, automation and self-hosted services. I also take part in CTFs and research hackathons.

HomeLab

Dedicated server running dual Intel Xeon E5-2680v4 CPUs with 64 GB of RAM, managed with Proxmox VE. Over 10 VMs and containers in production, ZFS storage and a VLAN-segmented network.

Current stack:

  • Pi-hole — DNS filtering + ad blocking
  • Nginx Proxy Manager — reverse proxy with wildcard SSL
  • WireGuard VPN — secure remote access
  • Jellyfin — media server
  • Immich — self-hosted photo management
  • N8N — workflow automation
  • fail2ban — SSH & service protection
